Basic Information

Real Name: David
Codename/Aliases: Solid Snake, Snake, Old Snake (in MGS4), Iroquois Pliskin (alias in MGS2), briefly others.
Nationality: American
Birth: 1972 (via cloning project)
Occupation: Former Green Beret, FOXHOUND operative, spy, special operations soldier, mercenary. Later anti-Metal Gear activist with Philanthropy.
Notable Titles: Legendary Soldier, Legendary Hero, "The Man Who Makes the Impossible Possible."
Creator: Hideo Kojima (Konami Metal Gear series).
Design: Consistent modern design by Yoji Shinkawa (starting with Metal Gear Solid). Earlier games referenced Hollywood action archetypes (e.g., similar to Snake Plissken).
Voice Actors: Akio Ōtsuka (Japanese), David Hayter (English, primary and iconic).

Origins and Genetics (Les Enfants Terribles)
Solid Snake is a clone of Big Boss (Naked Snake/John), created in the 1972 U.S. government project Les Enfants Terribles. The project aimed to produce the perfect soldier using Big Boss's genes.

Clones: Twin brother Liquid Snake (recessive/dominant gene debate varies by game perspective); Solidus Snake (third, "perfect" clone).
Process: Somatic cell nuclear transfer using Big Boss's DNA. Surrogate mother EVA. Clones given accelerated aging genes to limit their lifespan and prevent rebellion or enemy capture. Solid Snake inherited mitochondrial DNA from the Japanese egg donor.
Early Life: Raised under various foster parents in the U.S. Real name and origins classified. Joined the military young, served as a Green Beret (including Gulf War involvement in 1990-1991).

Personality and Traits
Solid Snake is a hardened, stoic veteran who buries emotions during missions. He appears calm, collected, intense, and sometimes rude or cynical, but possesses a deeply human, philosophical side.

Key Characteristics: High intelligence (IQ 180), fluent in six languages. Expert in stealth, espionage, combat. Loves life despite battlefield existence; believes in passing on experiences (speech, art, emotions) beyond genes. Flirtatious at times, self-sacrificing, values friendship and love even in war. Rejects glory in violence but is highly effective at killing. Struggles with PTSD, loneliness, and identity as a clone.
Philosophy: Rejects being a tool of governments or fate. Emphasizes individual choice, legacy through memories/culture, and living as a "man" rather than a "snake." Famous for lines like: "Life isn't just about passing on your genes..." and "I'm no hero. Never was, never will be."
Weaknesses/Habits: Smoker (despite health warnings), musher in downtime, occasional sarcasm/dry humor.

Notable Abilities:

Master of CQC (Close Quarters Combat, trained by Big Boss).
Expert in infiltration, marksmanship, survival, piloting, free climbing, diving, skydiving.
Perceptive tactician and improviser. In MGS4, uses OctoCamo and FaceCamo.
Enhanced genetics grant peak human (or superior) strength, agility, resilience—though accelerated aging affects him later.

Mission Timeline (Core Canon)

1995: Outer Heaven Uprising (Metal Gear): Rookie FOXHOUND mission. Infiltrates Outer Heaven (South Africa), destroys TX-55 Metal Gear, defeats (Venom) Big Boss.
Early 2000s: Zanzibar Land Disturbance (Metal Gear 2: Solid Snake): Rescues Dr. Kio Marv, destroys Metal Gear D, defeats Gray Fox and true Big Boss. Goes into semi-retirement afterward.
2005: Shadow Moses Incident (Metal Gear Solid / The Twin Snakes): Stops FOXHOUND revolt led by Liquid Snake. Destroys Metal Gear REX. Learns clone origins.
2007-2009: Tanker/Big Shell Incidents (Metal Gear Solid 2): Tanker mission with Philanthropy (anti-Metal Gear group with Otacon). Assists Raiden on Big Shell as Pliskin. Confronts Solidus Snake. Fakes death after smear campaign.
2014: Guns of the Patriots (Metal Gear Solid 4): As "Old Snake" (accelerated aging). Final mission against Liquid Ocelot/Patriots. Destroys AI control system. Reconciles with Big Boss. Chooses peaceful retirement.

Key Relationships:

Big Boss: Genetic "father"/clone source. Complex mentor-pupil, betrayal, and eventual reconciliation.
Liquid Snake & Solidus Snake: Brothers/clones. Rivalry, especially with Liquid.
Otacon (Hal Emmerich): Close friend and Philanthropy partner.
Meryl Silverburgh: Romantic interest (varies by canon).
Roy Campbell: Commander/mentor figure.
Others: Naomi Hunter, Mei Ling, Raiden (ally), Gray Fox (rival/mentor figure).

Supporting Elements: FOXHOUND service, repeated Metal Gear destruction preventing nuclear threats, battles against The Patriots' control.
Appearances and Media

Main Games: Metal Gear (1987), Metal Gear 2: Solid Snake, Metal Gear Solid series (1-4).
Spin-offs/Other: Ghost Babel, Acid series (parallel), audio dramas, novels, comics. Cameos/allusions in prequels (Portable Ops, Peace Walker, Ground Zeroes, Phantom Pain).
Crossovers: Playable in Super Smash Bros. series, Fortnite, etc.
Non-canon/Related: Snake's Revenge (NES, non-Kojima).

Legacy: Iconic stealth-action protagonist. Symbol of anti-war themes, personal agency, and legacy in Kojima's narrative. Praised for depth, voice acting, and philosophical monologues.
